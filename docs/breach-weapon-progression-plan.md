# Next pass — weapons as earned discoveries

Status: design direction, not implemented in the music-focused 0.8.4 update.

The player technically starts with a pistol, but early pickups can still feel
like a pile of equipment. Hollow places its scattergun and Rebound near the
spawn (46,100 and 43,100; spawn 48.5,102.5). Glasshouse teaches Rebound in its
early practice bay. A campaign should space these discoveries across chapters
and make new equipment change the player's understanding of earlier routes.

| Tool | Discovery to earn | Safe first lesson | Later payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pistol | Starting kit | Aim, cover and ammo discipline | Reliable fallback throughout |
| Scattergun | A defended security store after the first route loop | A close encounter with retreat space | Shortcuts through dangerous close quarters |
| Arc caster | Restore a remote workshop, then reach its equipment bay | A visible charge target with no time pressure | A proposed power-routing interaction that opens a previously seen route |
| Rebound carbine | An optional maintenance detour after its reflected-shot puzzle is introduced | A safe marked bank shot with recoverable supplies | A proposed around-corner latch and protected enemies |
| Thumper | A later machinery chamber, reached with earlier tools | A safe blast chamber with clear fuse feedback | A proposed brittle obstruction and optional return route |

Each discovery needs a distinct sequence: notice a barrier, explore, earn the
tool, learn it safely, recognize where it helps, then open a useful return.
Do not merely move all four pickups into the next room. Reward curiosity and
mastery with choices; keep essential goals reachable without perfect aim or
spending the last round at the wrong time. Proposed traversal interactions
above require actual game mechanics and readable art before weapon gates ship.

Standalone mission selection must still work: use a chapter-appropriate kit
and a short contextual refresher, while campaign mode carries earned unlocks.
Choose and test that policy before relocating existing tutorial pickups.
Existing saves need migration; checkpoint retries retain discoveries without
duplicating rewards. In co-op, permanent unlocks should belong to the team,
while equipped weapons and ammunition remain personal. Validate every route
with either player downed and with ammunition exhausted at a gate.

Keep pickup explanations and the first safe practice quiet. A musical reward
should follow demonstrated mastery or arrival in the newly opened section,
not bury the discovery text or trigger automatically on every weapon pickup.
