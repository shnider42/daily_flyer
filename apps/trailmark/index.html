    TRAILMARK / FIELD PILOT 0.1

    Know when to look up.

    Trailmark is a companion for the moment a path splits and the signs stop helping. Four Ponds in Bourne is the first area.

    What this pilot does

    Shows your location and reported accuracy, compares it with a selected mapped route, calls attention to checkpoints, and warns after repeated good GPS readings suggest you have left that route.

    What still needs a walk

    These routes are based on published mapping, not a field survey. Junction positions, signs, access and GPS behavior need to be checked on the ground. Use the official map and posted signs; this pilot does not establish whether terrain is passable.

    Why the screen stays on

    A browser cannot reliably monitor location with your phone locked. This version pauses when hidden and waits for a fresh fix when you return. Reliable background alerts require native location support.

    Sources & map notes

      Map labels and route distances are approximate. A line on the map is not proof of current public access. No location tracking starts until you choose Start GPS hike.
