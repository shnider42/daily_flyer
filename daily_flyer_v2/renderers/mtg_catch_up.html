A first edition, with room to grow.

16 selected milestones from June 2009 through September 22, 2026, plus an announced 2027 change. Coverage is strongest on broad rules and format changes. It is sparse between milestones.

Not yet covered: a full set-by-set history, every ban, competitive deck trends, prices, tournament results, and most individual cards. Modern and Pioneer coverage is introductory.

Reviewed facts; visible interpretation.

Each change links to an official Wizards source. “Why it matters” is our explanation. Announcement dates and effective dates are separate. An old announcement is not a current legality check.
