THE SETTING

Mogadishu, 1993.

On 3–4 October 1993, a U.S. raid in Mogadishu became a prolonged rescue operation after two Black Hawk helicopters were shot down. The battle took place amid Somalia’s civil conflict and a wider humanitarian and political crisis.

The overnight relief effort included U.S., Malaysian, and Pakistani forces. Somali civilians suffered in the fighting. The city was home to people, not simply a battlefield.

CHALK is a fictionalized game, not a reconstruction. The four-person squad, names, dialogue, maps, sequence lengths, and outcomes are invented or compressed. It does not reproduce the film’s script or depict individual historical soldiers.

The missions emphasize squad survival, rescue, civilian protection, and withdrawal. Civilians are unarmed, AI squad members do not target them, and civilian harm is recorded.

Desktop aiming and settings

Point with the mouse and hold the left button to fire. The camera stays centered by default. In Settings → Aim & controls, increase Look toward aim if you prefer the view to extend toward your cursor. Settings also includes crosshair choices, video quality, audio levels, and HUD size. Press F3 during play to show or hide performance information.

On a phone

Place your left thumb on the movement pad and drag to move. Push to its outer edge to sprint. A light drag on the right stick aims; push farther to fire. Your weapon reloads automatically when empty. Assist appears when there is someone or something nearby to help—hold it to work. Tap Squad for orders, or Radio when following the squad leader. Choose Move, then tap your destination on the map. Portrait and landscape are both supported.

Play with a friend

Choose Create co-op squad, then share the room code or invite link. Your friend chooses Join squad. Two people control Hale and Mercer; Ames and Webb stay under computer control. The room host can swap your roles in the briefing or in the mission menu. A menu does not pause an online mission. Disconnected soldiers return to AI control; reconnect promptly to reclaim your place.

Your two roles

Lead: play as Hale and give the squad destinations and priorities. Follow: play as Mercer while Hale leads the mission, announces intentions, and waits if you fall behind. You can switch roles from pause.

Progress and preferences stay in this browser. A solo mission restarts if you reload the page. An online session attempts to reconnect. No account is needed.
