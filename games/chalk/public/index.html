      THE SETTING

      Mogadishu, 1993.

      On 3–4 October 1993, a U.S. raid in Mogadishu became a prolonged rescue operation after two Black Hawk helicopters were shot down. The battle took place amid Somalia’s civil conflict and a wider humanitarian and political crisis.

      The initial raid had a planned ground extraction. Helicopter losses, blocked routes, casualties, and difficult communications forced repeated changes to the rescue effort.

      The overnight relief effort included U.S., Malaysian, and Pakistani forces. Somali civilians suffered in the fighting. The city was home to people, not simply a battlefield.

      CHALK is a fictionalized game, not a reconstruction. The four-person squad, names, dialogue, maps, sequence lengths, and outcomes are invented or compressed. The radio decisions and resident encounters are fictional composites, not claims about choices made by named soldiers. It does not reproduce the film’s script or depict individual historical soldiers. The six standalone field operations are fictional scenarios, outside the four-chapter campaign.

      The missions emphasize squad survival, rescue, civilian protection, and withdrawal. Civilians are unarmed, AI squad members do not target them, and civilian harm is recorded.

      During play, you receive the next confirmed order rather than a guarantee of how the operation will end. Choices can cost supplies or time, change your route, or affect later chapters. Helping a resident is not a promise of a tactical reward.

      FIELD GUIDE

      Stay together. Know your next step.

      Follow the amber diamond to your next objective. Your squad fights alongside you. Bring everyone to the pickup point.

      What the screen is telling you

      Objective
      The task at the upper left. The amber diamond shows where to go. The bar fills as you do the task.
      Squad
      Your four soldiers and their health. On mobile, open Squad or Radio to see them. The highlighted name is you. Select a free standing soldier to take control. In co-op, your partner's soldier stays theirs.
      Health
      How badly you are hurt. A low number means you need cover and help. A downed soldier can still be rescued while alive.
      Ammunition
      The large number is rounds loaded in your weapon. The smaller number is spare ammunition. Reloading moves spare rounds into the weapon.
      Cover
      A wall or solid object that blocks bullets. An open doorway leaves you exposed. Walk through doorways to enter buildings.
      Suppression
      Incoming fire makes aiming harder. Get behind solid cover and let the pressure fall.
      Sight and fog
      Bright ground is visible to your squad now. Dim ground is somewhere you have seen before. Dark ground has not been seen. Walls block sight; a ping does not reveal what is there.
      Ping
      A blue marker that means “look here.” It does not move the squad or reveal hidden enemies. Both players can place one, including on the map.
      Radio choice
      A decision about the mission. The leader chooses; a squad member can recommend. Read the time remaining—play keeps moving.

      Your controls

      These match your saved bindings. Change any gameplay shortcut in Settings → Aim & controls. Escape always cancels or opens the mission menu.

      Orders and pings

      Move sends the squad to a point. Regroup brings them back to you. Hold keeps them in place. Assist sends help to the wounded. If you are following Hale, those buttons request Ready, Wait, or Help instead.

      Ping only points something out. Aim your cursor and press Middle mouse. A map ping points at the same place on the battlefield.

      Equipment, in plain words

      Smoke hides movement for a short time. Frag explodes and can hurt anyone nearby. Flash briefly blinds people who can see its burst; it causes no direct health damage. Walls shield these effects. Check your squad and civilians before throwing.

      Select a type with Y or its equipment button; aim and press N to throw. Mercer's 40 mm launcher is separate. His binoculars let you look farther but slow movement.

      Four soldiers

      Hale leads. Mercer carries binoculars and the launcher. Ames is the medic. Webb carries the machine gun; its large belt takes longer to reload.

      Friendly support

      In Cordon, open Squad on mobile (Radio when following Hale) or use the desktop orders panel. Choose Request support here, then select a location on the map. The relief team travels there and reports its progress. One commitment is available; another friendly squad may already need it. A normal ping keeps current orders. When you reach a wounded ally, hold Assist to help them recover.

      On a phone

      Drag the left stick to move; push it farther to sprint. The right stick starts where you touch: aim inside the dashed ring, push into the outer band to fire, and pull back to stop firing while keeping your aim. Lifting your thumb stops firing immediately. Change Distance to fire in Settings → Aim & controls if you need more or less travel.

      As leader, tap visible ground to move your squad. The small Regroup button brings them back to you; as a squad member, it becomes Ready. Open Squad (or Radio as a member) for soldier health, switching soldiers, other orders, support, and Ping a place. A ping only marks a location; tapping the overview map places a ping. Tap the objective arrow for its explanation.

      Reload stays above the left stick. Hold Assist when it appears. Open Gear to choose a grenade, then drag its throw button and release; drag back to cancel.

      Play with a friend

      Create a co-op squad and share the room code or invite link. Each person chooses one of the four soldiers. Click a free soldier in the briefing or on the squad cards during play. The other soldiers stay under computer control. A soldier claimed by your partner is labelled with their name. Menus and this guide do not pause an online mission.

      Preferences stay in this browser. Reloading restarts a solo mission; an online session attempts to reconnect.

      FIELD PREFERENCES

      Settings

      Changes apply immediately and stay in this browser.

      Video & performance

      Start with Balanced. Lower the resolution if a large display feels slow.

      Canvas 2D · Resolution updates with the game window.

      Aim & controls

      Adjust aiming cues, the camera, and how far the mobile aim stick travels before firing. Mouse aim follows your cursor directly.

      Keyboard & mouse buttons

      Click a binding, then press a key or a mouse button. Each action can have two shortcuts. Escape cancels an edit and always remains your way back to the mission menu.

      Sound mix

      Balance nearby action against wind, helicopter passes, radio traffic, and distant impacts.

      Sound starts after your first click or tap. The Sound switch mutes everything without changing this mix. The menu soundscape uses ambient field sounds.

      Screen help & text size

      The HUD is the information around the battlefield: your task, squad, health, and equipment.

      The introduction teaches one control at a time during play. You can skip any step or hide it. Changing guidance does not change combat difficulty.

      Your objective, health, ammunition, and wounded-soldier alerts stay visible. On mobile, open Squad or Radio to inspect all four soldiers.
