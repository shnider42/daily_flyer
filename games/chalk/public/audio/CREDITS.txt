Both command files retain the complete short spoken phrase, with band limiting
to 220–3,600 Hz, loudness adjustment and brief edge fades. Converted to mono
22,050 Hz / 64 kbps MP3. They are fictional performance assets, not historical
radio traffic. Menu playback keeps them sparse; mission playback uses them
only for corresponding movement orders, smoke movement calls, and a cleared
convoy route. Browser speech synthesis is not used.

Menu wind and helicopter layers reuse the recorded assets credited above.
Subdued distant impact/rumble and carrier-squelch layers are procedural sound
design made for CHALK. They are not recordings of actual explosions or voices.

fear-1.mp3 / fear-2.mp3
Source: 1yell2.wav / 2yell1.wav, from "Male Grunt/Yelling sounds"
Creator: HaelDB
https://opengameart.org/content/male-gruntyelling-sounds
Public archive:
https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/yelling%20sounds.zip
License selected: CC0 1.0, explicitly offered on the source page alongside
OGA-BY 3.0. The page describes four male vocal performances recorded on a
Neuman microphone and Avalon 2022 preamp. The two short vocalizations come
from different numbered performers. Their in-game fear/alarm context is
fictional sound design, not a claim about an actual emergency or the battle.

Processing for all four files: remove leading/trailing silence with a short
safety margin; high-pass at 85 Hz and low-pass at 8 kHz; normalize peak to
approximately -4 dBFS; apply 5 ms opening and 15 ms closing fades; convert to
mono 24,000 Hz, 64 kbps MP3. Active edited durations before MP3 padding:
pain-1 0.264 seconds; pain-2 0.410 seconds; fear-1 0.781 seconds;
fear-2 0.518 seconds. Playback applies distance, stereo placement and room
filtering. No archival injury or 1993 victim audio is used.

Cloth/gear bullet impacts, the player's low impact thud, and the restrained
near-wreck fire/crackle bed are procedural sound design made for CHALK.
They are separate from the performed voices and are not injury recordings.
