Both command files retain the complete short spoken phrase, with band limiting
to 220–3,600 Hz, loudness adjustment and brief edge fades. Converted to mono
22,050 Hz / 64 kbps MP3. They are fictional performance assets, not historical
radio traffic. Menu playback keeps them sparse; mission playback uses them
only for corresponding movement orders, smoke movement calls, and a cleared
convoy route. Browser speech synthesis is not used.

Menu wind and helicopter layers reuse the recorded assets credited above.
Subdued distant impact/rumble and carrier-squelch layers are procedural sound
design made for CHALK. They are not recordings of actual explosions or voices.
