THE FIELD GUIDE

ONE PITCH AT A TIME.

01

AT THE PLATE

Aim with the left pad or arrow keys. Tap SWING or press Space as the ball reaches the plate. POWER / Shift trades a smaller contact window for a harder hit. Let pitches outside the zone go.

02

ON THE MOUND

Pick a pitch, aim, then tap PITCH. Tap again when the marker reaches the gold target. Better release timing means better location. Mix fastballs and breaking pitches.

03

IN PLAY

Your fielders chase the ball and your runners advance automatically. On a ground ball, tap THROW TO 1ST to make an early throw, or let your fielder finish the play. Three outs changes sides.

HOW MUCH HELP?

Simple adds a pitch timing guide, strike zone, and contextual tips. Moderate keeps the aim guide and shorter tips. Expert leaves the essentials. Difficulty separately controls CPU skill and timing forgiveness.

You play Boston Harbor, the visiting team. Exhibition starts with you batting. Practice keeps you at the plate. Touch and keyboard both work; landscape gives you more field.
