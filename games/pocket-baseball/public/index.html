THE FIELD GUIDE

ONE PITCH AT A TIME.

01

AT THE PLATE

Aim with the left pad or arrow keys. Tap SWING or press Space as the ball reaches the plate. POWER / Shift trades a smaller contact window for a harder hit. Let pitches outside the zone go.

02

ON THE MOUND

Each pitcher has a different repertoire. Pick a pitch with its button or 1–4, aim, then tap PITCH. Tap again in the gold target for a better release. The knuckleballer trades speed and command for flutter, and can leave a hittable pitch hanging. Choose the opponent pitcher on the home screen to face that repertoire in practice.

03

MAKE THE THROW

Fielders chase the ball. You choose the play: tap 1ST, 2ND, 3RD, or HOME to queue a throw before pickup, or throw from the fielder’s hand. Keyboard 1–4 matches the bases, with 4 = home. Change your choice before release, or queue the next throw while the ball travels. A wrong base can let a runner escape.

04

STRETCH THE HIT

The runner’s destination stays above the buttons. As soon as the batter heads to first, GO 2ND queues the attempt at a double—even before first is reached. Each press of E / Space sends the selected runner one base beyond the current destination. HOLD / Q stops at the next reachable bag; RETURN / B goes back to the last touched bag when legal. Forced runners must advance. Tap a runner’s name, or press R, to choose among runners.

HOW MUCH HELP?

Simple adds timing, aim, explanations and a suggested throw. Moderate keeps the aim guide and shorter tips. Expert leaves the essentials. Difficulty separately controls CPU skill and timing forgiveness. Field movement is assisted; your throw and running decisions affect the play. Steals and tag-ups are outside this version.

You play Boston Harbor, the visiting team. Exhibition starts with you batting. Practice keeps you at the plate and lets you work on stretching hits. Touch and keyboard both work; landscape gives you more field.
