THE FIELD GUIDE

ONE PITCH AT A TIME.

01

AT THE PLATE

Aim with the left pad or arrow keys. Tap SWING or press Space as the ball reaches the plate. POWER / Shift trades a smaller contact window for a harder hit. Let pitches outside the zone go.

02

ON THE MOUND

Each pitcher has a different repertoire. Pick a pitch with its button or 1–4, aim, then tap PITCH. Tap again in the gold target for a better release. The knuckleballer trades speed and command for flutter, and can leave a hittable pitch hanging. Choose the opponent pitcher on the home screen to face that repertoire in practice.

03

MAKE THE THROW

Fielders chase the ball. You choose the play: tap 1ST, 2ND, 3RD, or HOME to queue a throw before pickup, or throw from the fielder’s hand. Keyboard 1–4 matches the bases, with 4 = home. Change your choice before release, or queue the next throw while the ball travels. A wrong base can let a runner escape.

04

STRETCH THE HIT

The runner’s destination stays above the buttons. As soon as the batter heads to first, GO 2ND queues the attempt at a double—even before first is reached. Each press of E / Space sends the selected runner one base beyond the current destination. HOLD / Q stops at the next reachable bag; RETURN / B goes back to the last touched bag when legal. Forced runners must advance. Tap a runner’s name, or press R, to choose among runners.

05

TAKE A LEAD. PICK YOUR MOMENT.

With a runner on base, select their base in the small ON BASE card. LEAD + / X moves farther off the bag; BACK / Z shortens the lead. A longer lead helps a steal but gives the pitcher a pickoff chance. STEAL / C queues a jump on the delivery; tap again before the windup to cancel. You can also send the runner during the delivery. Slow pitches give the runner more time.

06

KEEP THEM HONEST

Before pitching, use the HOLD THE RUNNERS card to pick off first, second, or third. You get two pickoff attempts per batter. After a steal, choose a base for the catcher’s throw. The runner can use RETURN to dive back during a pickoff. The ball and runner must actually reach the play.

07

TAG UP ON A CATCH

A caught fly puts the batter out, but runners can still advance. They must touch their original bag after the catch. TAG & GO queues the return and the next base; TAG UP sends a runner who has already retouched. A throw back to the original bag can double off a runner who has not returned. A deep fly can score a run with fewer than two outs.

08

THE BALLPARK

Choose Fenway-inspired for the tall left-field wall, deep center-field triangle and short right-field corner. A drive below the wall stays in play; an airborne ball must clear its local wall height for a home run. A ball that bounces over a low fence receives a ground-rule double. The original Harbor Field remains available. Your selection applies to exhibition and both practice modes.

09

PLAY A FRIEND

Choose your pitcher, then open PLAY A FRIEND. One player creates a room and shares its six-character code; the other joins. Both tap READY TO PLAY. The creator chooses the ballpark and plays Boston; the guest plays Brooklyn. Each player confirms between plays. A disconnect pauses the match while the player reconnects. Use REJOIN YOUR MATCH in the same tab after a refresh.

HOW MUCH HELP?

Simple adds timing, aim, explanations and a suggested throw. Moderate keeps the aim guide and shorter tips. Expert leaves the essentials. Difficulty separately controls CPU skill and timing forgiveness. Field movement is assisted; your throw and running decisions affect the play.

Solo exhibition starts with you batting as Boston Harbor. Batting practice keeps you at the plate; baserunning practice starts with runners on first and third. Touch and keyboard both work; landscape gives you more field. Online rooms are temporary and close when a player leaves or the match server restarts.
