Keep it up.

01 / FIND THE BALL The ring on the sand shows where the ball is heading. Move there before it drops.

02 / BUILD THE RALLY Hold Pass / Set as the ball reaches you. Your first two touches build with your teammate; your third sends it over.

03 / FINISH IT Hold Spike for an attacking shot. Move across the court while hitting to aim. Dive / Block stretches for a save or challenges a ball near the net.
