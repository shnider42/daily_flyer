The house rules

  • The table stays open after the break. Your first legal pot after that assigns your group.
  • Hit your group first, then pocket a ball or reach a cushion. On an open table, hit a solid or stripe first.
  • A scratch, wrong first ball, or shot without a cushion or pocket gives your opponent ball in hand. Tap an empty spot to place the cue ball, then aim.
  • On the break, pocket a ball or send four object balls to cushions. An 8-ball on the break is re-spotted.
  • Pocketing the 8-ball early, in the wrong called pocket, or with a foul loses the rack. Clear your group before taking your 8-ball shot.
