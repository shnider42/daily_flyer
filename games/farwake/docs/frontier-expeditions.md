# Farwake frontier expeditions

Version 0.31.0 · World content revision 5

For the later Morrow corridor format, optional Earth evidence and continuity constraints, read [Quiet Earth canon and corridor authoring](quiet-earth-canon.md) before extending that storyline. The systems below remain its underlying expedition framework.

## Scope

Fifty encounter archetypes are available: the existing Hearth cavern family and 49 new authored frontier stories. The new stories use 16 physical layout families, with different evidence, repair sequences, choices, residents, creatures, battles and persistent consequences.

Each new archetype has a guaranteed home on the existing chart. Eligible worlds elsewhere select a stable, biome-appropriate mixture from the same catalog. This changes the former repeating hollow, empty shelter and field relay slots while retaining existing named quest locations. Hearth, Glimmer, Crosswind, military bases and preflight cultures keep their established encounters.

## Finding and completing a story

Open the galaxy chart or current-system chart and expand **On the surface**. Land on the named body, open its local chart, and mark the relevant trail or entrance. The entry briefing describes the situation before you enter. Exterior locations use **Follow trail** and **Return to landing trails**.

Inside a story, the field objective and local chart show the current work. **Mark next step** points to the required physical record or control. A record must actually be read before it satisfies an evidence requirement. Repair sequences accept one action at a time, retain partial progress, and explain when a wrong sequence resets. Residents discuss their own situation and its recorded outcome.

## Geography and inhabitants

The new layout families are: bridgefront, trenches, crashfield, tidal-reef, quarry, fungal-grove, courtyard, archive-yard, powerplant, clinic-camp, creature-nest, ice-fissure, antenna-array, flooded-ruins, observatory, convoy-road. They provide real differences in walkable routes, water, obstacles, cover and lines of sight. Exterior maps have open terrain edges; the dedicated cave retains enclosed rock boundaries. Existing Hearth cave geometry is unchanged.

The eight added wildlife or machine silhouettes are reedstalker, mire-spitter, shardling, sporeback, glassmaw, ember-mite, prism-sentinel and sentry-drone. Their authored encounters exercise fast melee, ranged spit, sentinel, pack, raider, rifle and support profiles. Human, Choral, Vaeli, Keth and Oru residents appear as workers, neighbors, delegates, researchers and rescuers, as well as armed faction members where a dispute calls for them.

## Battles and consequences

Opposing local squads can move and fight one another while remaining neutral toward a visiting captain. Assisted aim selects hostiles. Deliberate manual aim can strike a neutral combatant; an actual hit makes that local faction hostile toward the offending captain. Peaceful conversation residents are not combat targets. Negotiated ceasefires can stop local fighting, and evacuation or treatment decisions change resident presentation.

Each story records its evidence, sequence progress, settled choices, claimed caches, combatant health and local aftermath. Shared-galaxy decisions use the existing authoritative server state: two captains cannot collect the same final reward twice. Outcomes and partial work survive a server reload. Reputation changes respect existing standing limits. Each decision's aftermath remains local to that site.

## Camp and tone

The recruitment demonstration, victory rehearsal and escaped exhibition-vermin stories supply occasional military bureaucracy satire. Rare frontier conversation and ambient notices include a Starship Troopers movie-night joke, missing commemorative mugs, recruitment-film promises and impractical safety classifications. Generic selection gives satire weight 2 and rare horror weight 1 against ordinary stories at weight 10; the authored showcase destinations remain available. The weighting is a selection bias, not a fixed percentage of every voyage.

Camp dialogue is excluded from Hearth's authored cast, urgent conversations, active expedition dialogue and preflight cultures. Alien peoples are not treated as a single hostile species: many of the new disputes involve shared services, testimony, labor, translation, ecology or custody.

## Save and deployment compatibility

- Existing entrance coordinates and the old two/three-site footprint rule are retained so settlement services and paid homes do not move. The planet layout version is unchanged.
- New stories have new IDs. A captain saved inside an old generic hollow, shelter or relay can still finish and leave through the legacy lookup.
- The galaxy seed, chart coordinates, ship ownership and shared-galaxy codes are retained.
- World content revision 5 requires an older connected client to refresh before joining the updated shared authority.
- Version 0.30.0's two-captain hulls and Casual NPC reserve behavior remain in place.
