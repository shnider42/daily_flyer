YOUR FIRST DROP

A SMALL ISLAND.
A BIG PROBLEM.

  1. 01

    LAND SOMEWHERE GOOD.

    Steer your glider toward a landmark. Everyone has a starter rifle, so find cover before looking for better gear.

  2. 02

    GEAR UP. GET MOVING.

    Open gold caches. Collect weapons, shields, and supplies. Rarer gear hits harder. Use launch pads to clear the hills.

  3. 03

    MAKE YOUR OWN COVER.

    Build a wall with Q or a ramp with C. Each costs 10 materials. Salvage nearby trees, rocks, or crates with F.

  4. 04

    WATCH THE CIRCLE.

    The purple storm keeps closing. The white circle on your map shows where to go. Outlast the other nine to win.
