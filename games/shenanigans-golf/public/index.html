A quick introduction

Read the green.
Question everything else.

  1. Choose your line. Click or touch the turf where you want to aim. The dotted line shows the direction of your shot.
  2. Set the power. Move the power slider. A little goes a long way near the cup.
  3. Putt. Press the big button or Space. Wait for the ball to stop before shooting again.

A fair contest, in an unfair place.

Everyone plays at the same time. Balls pass through other golfers’ balls. Computers take real shots through the same obstacles.

Water, lava and falling off cost a penalty stroke and return you to your last safe lie. Each hole has a 12-stroke cap and a shared 2½-minute clock; an unfinished hole scores 12. Then everyone moves on together.

Stroke play: fewest strokes over 18 holes wins. Points: each hole awards 4, 3, 2 and 1 points; tied players split the points for their places. Highest total wins. Tied rounds are shared wins.

A disconnected online player is covered by a computer. Rejoin in the same browser tab to reclaim your place. Live rooms last for this session; a server restart ends them.
