Controls

Choose a play and press Space. Tap 1–4 or click a receiver to lob. Hold the key or button briefly for a bullet. On runs, follow your blocks and press E to cut. On defense, E switches defenders and Space plays the ball or dives for a tackle. Use the stick and action buttons on touchscreens. Drag the field map to move the camera, then choose Follow play to resume tracking.
