Suggestion Box

A quieter way to turn player feedback into a reviewable shortlist.

Private first. Published only after review.

Submitting is not publishing. Suggestions stay private until moderator screening, verified reviewer clearance of the exact wording, and explicit publication approval are complete.

This is a community-run site, not an official Team17 support channel. No Team17 review arrangement or endorsement is claimed by this pilot setup.

Bulletin Board

No suggestions have been published. Only entries cleared through the required process will appear here.

How this pilot works

Private submission → moderation → verified reviewer clearance → publication → community voting.

Up to 20 invited participants, two submissions each, 15 submissions total, and a shortlist of at most five. A published batch gets a seven-day voting window.

One removable upvote per invited account, per published version. Community support is not bug severity, a developer roadmap, or proof that a defect exists. Votes only rank suggestions that were cleared for publication.

No response is not approval. New wording requires new clearance; a moderator rewrite also needs the submitter’s acceptance. Withdrawing removes the entry from the public board. Duplicate entries are linked privately; votes are not silently combined.

Privacy and participation

During the pilot, your sign-in email is handled by the authentication provider. It is not displayed on the board or included in the reviewer shortlist. Your private submission is visible to you and authorized moderators; the assigned reviewer receives shortlisted text without account identifiers.

Published entries show only approved wording, the agreed review attribution, review date, optional public response, related issue link, and aggregate votes. Private correspondence and voter identities are never published.

Do not submit personal information, credentials, harassment, private correspondence, or exploit instructions. This is not an urgent support or security-reporting channel. Keep private feedback anonymous within the text.

You can withdraw a submission from My submissions. Withdrawal hides it from this site; it cannot retract copies already made by others. A retention/deletion procedure and a participant contact address must be configured before invitations are issued.

Signing out clears this page’s private views. Keep me signed in is optional. Open tabs can share a session; other browsers and devices require their own login. Your submissions are not stored in browser storage.
