    BEFORE YOU GO IN

    STAY MOVING.

    1. Find your feet.

      Use WASD to move, your mouse to turn, and click to fire. Use 1–8 or the mouse wheel to select carried weapons. Press F for fullscreen. Arrow keys turn too. If mouse capture is unavailable, hold and drag to aim while firing.

      Move with the left pad and drag the open view to look. Safe mode is the default: tap FIRE for one shot, or hold it still briefly to fire continuously. Dragging immediately from FIRE only looks. After a deliberate hold starts firing, you can drag to track a target. Tap a gun in the bottom weapon selector to equip it. Change thumb size or choose Classic fire-and-drag in Settings.

    2. Use the facility.

      Walk over supplies. Press E or tap the highlighted action near a switch or door. Simple explains blocked doors; Moderate gives sensory hints; Expert leaves you to read the world. Actions appear when available.

    3. Find a way out.

      Follow the current objective. Restore power and recover access; Dead Relay and Crosslink also require both relay consoles. The map shows areas you’ve visited. In The Underworks, new equipment opens earlier routes; refuges hold checkpoints and active transit links shorten the return trip. The expanded map pauses solo play. In co-op it stays live, so find cover before inspecting discovered sectors, gates, and stations. The Switchyard is a solo mission: matching circuit numbers connect shutter controls to their doors, and the arc caster rewards close, careful shots. The Glasshouse supports solo or co-op through its overgrown research galleries. The Hollow is a larger expedition: numbered routing cabinets trade containment fields for shortcuts. Beneath The Shadow adds earned weapons, multiplying roots and optional gantry jumps: face the landing and use the green launch beacon. Record terminals add optional story entries to the pause-menu field journal.

    4. Go in together.

      Crosslink, The Underworks, The Glasshouse, The Hollow, and Beneath The Shadow support private co-op. Maps stay live. When both operators enter menus the world pauses; either returning resumes it. A disconnect or backgrounded app pauses the team until both choose Resume. Downed operators return at the next checkpoint. Rooms end when someone leaves or the service restarts.

    5. Make the run yours.

      Simple guidance teaches you as you go. You can change guidance or choose Compact, Standard, or Large thumb controls in Settings at any time. Combat difficulty is separate.
