A CAMPAIGN THROUGH THE BREACH

DESIGN PROPOSAL

Seven connected chapters proposed, with Beneath The Shadow available as a separate standalone expedition: you start by restoring a damaged facility. You end by questioning whether restoring its connections helped the thing inside it.

The soundtrack and listening room are playable now. The connecting story, persistent campaign progress and final choice below are proposed additions, not an implemented campaign mode.

ACT I / RESTORE CONTACT

You think the silence is a fault.

  1. Blacksite 07

    A contained rescue becomes an escape. Iron Wake sets the opening pace, then gives way to the facility’s sounds. Leave the extraction quiet.

  2. Dead Relay

    Bring the relays back online. Propose the first contradiction in the recovered signal: an evacuation acknowledgment predating your request. Dead Carrier gets one quiet vault moment.

  3. Crosslink

    Confirm the route beneath the network. A short, concentrated operation makes room for partnership. Dry Contact introduces the chapter; the vault has its own placed cue.

ACT II / FOLLOW THE CONNECTIONS

The machine was built to keep something here.

  1. The Underworks

    The campaign opens into a longer expedition. Equipment changes how old routes work. Crown Circuit belongs at the far crowns; Spare Air introduces the descent. Leave navigation, upgrades and refuge conversation unscored.

  2. Switchyard

    A shorter, physical chapter after the descent. Routing changes the shape of a fight. Counterweight introduces the chapter; cabinets and shutter experiments remain clear.

  3. The Glasshouse

    The network is sustaining an ecosystem. Learning shield and healing behavior comes first; Glass Teeth and Root Pressure provide contrasting later pressure. Propose evidence that restoration has also carried the signal farther.

ACT III / CHOOSE WHAT TO KEEP

The recording answers first.

  1. The Hollow

    The existing archive already describes replies arriving before questions. Let its records, containment tradeoffs and long approaches carry the chapter. Below the Carrier and The Room Replies are small exceptions. Weight of Stone may arrive only after the Choir Keeper’s first attack cycles.

Proposed ending: isolate the repeaters and preserve containment, or open an evacuation route at the cost of part of the isolation grid. Show the consequence through doors, lights and a final written report. Exit Load belongs only on a dangerous return. A safe exit needs no anthem.

Carry discoveries, chapter completion and the player’s decision forward. Keep each mission’s authored equipment progression. A future co-op campaign also needs mission parity and durable shared saves; today, four missions support private co-op.

Newly written note sequences. Procedural instruments. No source-song MIDI, sampled recordings or imitated vocals. Broad genre traits guide the palette; this is not a claim of legal clearance.
