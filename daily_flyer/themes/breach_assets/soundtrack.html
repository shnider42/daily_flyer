MUSIC CARRIES THE MISSION

Keep the pulse.

Every map has a continuing, authored set of tracks. Region themes follow the route at the next phrase; short crossfades keep them connected. Boss music sustains the encounter, and returning through cleared rooms still has a score. Enemy counts never shuffle the soundtrack.

Silence is a deliberate event: six seconds at Dead Relay’s arrival, eight at the Hollow dock, ten in its listening gallery, eight in the Underworks quarters, six in the Glasshouse practice bay, and ten in Shadow’s chapel. Each happens once and ends early if danger arrives. Records and warning sounds lower the mix; checkpoints, power and transit no longer stop it. Pausing and results still quiet the score. Live co-op maps keep the music.

The menu continuously alternates Containment Pulse and Lanterns Below after a tap. Use Settings to change music volume or turn it off.

A CAMPAIGN THROUGH THE BREACH

DESIGN PROPOSAL

Seven connected chapters proposed, with Beneath The Shadow available as a separate standalone expedition: you start by restoring a damaged facility. You end by questioning whether restoring its connections helped the thing inside it.

The soundtrack and listening room are playable now. The connecting story, persistent campaign progress and final choice below are proposed additions, not an implemented campaign mode.

ACT I / RESTORE CONTACT

You think the silence is a fault.

  1. Blacksite 07

    A contained rescue becomes an escape. Iron Wake sets the opening pace, then joins the recurring facility score. Exit Load carries the return.

  2. Dead Relay

    Bring the relays back online. Propose the first contradiction in the recovered signal: an evacuation acknowledgment predating your request. Dead Carrier runs through the chapter’s signal-themed rotation.

  3. Crosslink

    Confirm the route beneath the network. A short, concentrated operation makes room for partnership. Dry Contact introduces the chapter; the vault has its own placed cue.

ACT II / FOLLOW THE CONNECTIONS

The machine was built to keep something here.

  1. The Underworks

    The campaign opens into a longer expedition. Equipment changes how old routes work. Crown Circuit belongs at the far crowns; Spare Air introduces the descent. Use lighter themes for archives and quarters, and lower the mix for records.

  2. Switchyard

    A shorter, physical chapter after the descent. Routing changes the shape of a fight. Counterweight introduces the chapter; the score carries on through cabinets and shutter experiments at a clear mix.

  3. The Glasshouse

    The network is sustaining an ecosystem. Learning shield and healing behavior comes first; Glass Teeth and Root Pressure provide contrasting later pressure. Propose evidence that restoration has also carried the signal farther.

ACT III / CHOOSE WHAT TO KEEP

The recording answers first.

  1. The Hollow

    The existing archive already describes replies arriving before questions. Let its records, containment tradeoffs and long approaches carry the chapter. Below the Carrier and The Room Replies recur in the listening spaces. Weight of Stone sustains the Choir Keeper encounter after a brief local sighting.

Proposed ending: isolate the repeaters and preserve containment, or open an evacuation route at the cost of part of the isolation grid. Show the consequence through doors, lights and a final written report. Exit Load carries the return route, with the score fading for the final report.

Carry discoveries, chapter completion and the player’s decision forward. Keep each mission’s authored equipment progression. A future co-op campaign also needs mission parity and durable shared saves; today, five missions support private co-op.

Newly written note sequences. Procedural instruments. No source-song MIDI, sampled recordings or imitated vocals. Broad genre traits guide the palette; this is not a claim of legal clearance.
