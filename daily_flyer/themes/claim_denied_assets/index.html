    WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBORHOOD

    MAKE THEM WAIT.

    1. Watch the windows. Shoot executives outside before they strip the boards. Bullets pass through the gaps.
    2. Keep the house standing. Hold E / USE near a damaged window to rebuild boards for free. Repairing earns limited bonus credits.
    3. Spend the severance. Use the four marked supply cabinets. Ammo costs 120, first aid 200, the shotgun 600, the carbine 1000.
    4. Look after your neighbors. Hold E / USE beside a downed friend for three seconds. They bleed out after 28 seconds. Eliminated teammates return after a cleared wave.
    5. Survive the escalation. Faster VPs join at wave 3, armored auditors at 4, the Chairman every fifth wave. If everyone goes down, the night is over.

    Desktop: WASD, mouse look, click to fire, R reload, E use, Shift sprint, 1–3 weapons, Esc pause. Phone: left thumb moves, right side looks, separate FIRE / USE / RELOAD buttons. Landscape recommended. Friendly fire is off.
