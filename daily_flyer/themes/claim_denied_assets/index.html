    WELCOME TO THE NEIGHBORHOOD

    MAKE THEM WAIT.

    1. Watch the windows. Shoot executives outside before they strip the boards. Bullets pass through the gaps.
    2. Keep the house standing. Hold E / USE near a damaged window to rebuild boards for free. Repairing earns limited bonus credits.
    3. Spend the severance. Use the marked supply cabinets. Ammo costs 120, first aid 200, the shotgun 600, the carbine 1000. Fine Print SMG costs 750, Final Notice rifle 1400, Golden Parachute LMG 2000.
    4. Look after your neighbors. Hold E / USE beside a downed friend for three seconds. They bleed out after 28 seconds. Eliminated teammates return after a cleared wave.
    5. Survive the escalation. Faster VPs join at wave 3, armored auditors at 4, the Chairman every fifth wave. Later waves bring charging Debt Collectors, shielded Compliance Officers, ranged Legal Counsel, and healing Benefits Brokers. If everyone goes down, the night is over.
    6. Take the high ground. Executive Retreat has two staircases to the gallery. Enemies use them too. Shoot through the open atrium; solid floor slabs stop bullets.

    Desktop: WASD, mouse look, click to fire, R reload, E use, Shift sprint, 1–6 weapons, Esc pause. Phone: left thumb moves; drag the LOOK pad with your right thumb to aim.
