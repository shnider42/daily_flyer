TAKE THIS TO PRACTICE

The first rehearsal.

One hour. One recording. One next step.

  1. 10 minAgree on tuning, set levels, and warm up together.
  2. 20 minWork on one riff or song section. Decide the tempo and ending.
  3. 20 minPlay it through. Make a rough recording and listen back.
  4. 10 minPick what each person will practice. Set the next rehearsal.

A finished rough take gives you something concrete to improve.

THE STARTER NOTES

A little direction.

Find your people

Write a short band brief: your influences, instruments needed, location, and how often you want to rehearse. Share one riff or demo so people can hear the direction.

Make a song finishable

Give a riff a working title, tempo, and tuning. Sketch an intro, a main section, a contrasting section, and an ending. Record the whole idea before polishing each part.

Prepare for a first gig

Time a complete rehearsal set, including gaps. Confirm the slot length, load-in, soundcheck, backline, and who brings each piece of gear directly with the organizer.

Give people one useful link

A short bio, a representative recording, the lineup, confirmed shows, and a booking contact are enough for your first band page. Add those here, then download the page.
