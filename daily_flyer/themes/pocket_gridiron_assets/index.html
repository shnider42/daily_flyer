THE QUICK HUDDLE

Football. Thumb-sized.

  1. On offense, call a play, then tap SNAP at the bottom right. The blue line is the line of scrimmage. Reach the gold line for a fresh set of four downs.
  2. Run with your left thumb. Drag the stick. BURST gives you a short speed boost and one chance to shrug off a tackle.
  3. Pass with A, B or C. Buttons match the letters above receivers. You control the catcher immediately. Passing ends once you cross the blue line.
  4. On defense, choose coverage and get set. The CPU snaps after its cadence. Move before the snap, but stay on your side of the blue line. SWITCH selects the defender nearest the ball. DIVE commits to a tackle; a miss briefly slows you down. If blocked, that button becomes SHED to break free.

7-on-7 arcade rules: four 45-second quarters, clock runs during plays, touchdowns include an automatic extra point. Interceptions and defensive fumble recoveries can be returned for touchdowns. Possession flips the field so the ball carrier always runs upscreen. Fourth down offers a playable punt or field goal within 55 yards. Kickoffs open each half and follow scores. Tied games go to sudden-death overtime.

Special teams: Aim with the stick or A/B/C (left/center/right). Tap START METER, then KICK to set power. For incoming kicks, tap READY TO RETURN. The returner tracks the landing ring until you move the stick. On punts, B signals a fair catch. After the catch, run upfield and burst; when kicking, switch and dive to tackle. Missed catches can roll or be downed. Arcade touchbacks: kickoff at the 25, punt at the 20; kickoff out of bounds at the 40.

Blocking: Follow your linemen through the lane. Pass blockers form a pocket; run blockers and receivers take separate threats. Blocks wear out, so release the pass before protection breaks down.

Flags: Crossing the line at the snap is offside (5 yards); pre-snap contact is encroachment (5 yards, dead ball). A beaten pocket blocker can grab and draw holding (10 yards, repeat the down). Near a goal line, penalties use half the distance; holding in your own end zone is a safety. Live flags are settled after the play. When the choice is yours, ACCEPT is at bottom right and DECLINE is above it. The CPU chooses for its team. Offsetting fouls replay the down. This is a limited arcade penalty set; return holding and pass interference are not modeled yet.

Ball security: Catchable passes occasionally drop; defenders can tip passes that stay live until they hit the turf. Contact can cause a fumble, with extra risk on sacks, dives and bursts. Chase the loose ball with the stick; SWITCH still works on defense. Either team can recover. Own fourth-down recoveries are dead and cannot advance beyond the fumble spot. End-zone interceptions are automatic touchbacks at the 20.

Difficulty changes decisions: Rookie reacts later and guesses more. Pro reads situations and recent run/pass tendencies. All-pro reacts sooner, covers more precisely and takes better pursuit angles. Player speed and ball-outcome odds stay the same. There are ten offensive plays and nine defensive calls.

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move. Space to snap, burst, dive or operate the kick meter. 1–3 pass or aim a kick; 1 switches defenders; 2 calls a punt fair catch. P pauses. Switching tabs pauses automatically.
