THE QUICK HUDDLE

Football. Thumb-sized.

  1. Call a play, then snap. The blue line is the line of scrimmage. Reach the gold line for a fresh set of four downs.
  2. Run with your left thumb. Drag the stick. BURST gives you a short speed boost and one chance to shrug off a tackle.
  3. Pass with A, B or C. Buttons match the letters above receivers. You control the catcher immediately. Passing ends once you cross the blue line.
  4. Play defense too. SWITCH selects the defender nearest the ball. DIVE commits to a tackle; a miss briefly slows you down.

7-on-7 arcade rules: four 45-second quarters, clock runs during plays, touchdowns include an automatic extra point. Interceptions end the play; fourth down offers a punt or field goal within 55 yards. Tied games go to sudden-death overtime.

Desktop: WASD / arrows to move. Space to snap, burst or dive. 1–3 to pass; 1 switches defenders. P pauses. Switching tabs pauses automatically.
