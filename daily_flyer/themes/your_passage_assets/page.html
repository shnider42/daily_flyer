A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Good questions.

Do I need to know what career I want? +

No. Exploring possibilities and learning what matters to you are useful places to start. You can come with questions, a rough idea, or an upcoming transition.

Is this for students or for schools? +

Both. Passages offers individual mentorship for high school and college students, as well as work with schools and organizations. A conversation with Tim can help clarify what fits.

What happens to my practice notes? +

They stay in the current page until you choose “Save on this browser” or download them. Browser saves do not sync to other devices and are not sent to Tim. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to read them. “Clear this passage” deletes the current passage’s browser save and on-screen notes.

How do I start working with Tim? +

Use “Book with Tim” or “Book a conversation” to open Tim’s Calendly page. You can discuss your goals, questions, program options, and fees directly with him. Practice notes stay in your browser; booking takes place separately on Calendly.
