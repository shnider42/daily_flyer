The later result is an unweighted mean of three season values, not a game-weighted career average. Requiring all three observations selects survivors with recorded results. These profiles are not a random historical sample, and this is not a causal test or a validated forecast.

Sources, definitions & missing data

Each bowler links to their original PBA.com Career Stats table. The source tables can differ from PBA Tour annual leaderboards and award articles; we keep one source family instead of mixing their figures. “Profile titles” are not a national Tour title count.

The directory’s “current season” flag defines this snapshot’s roster, not a claim that everyone bowled in 2026. This is not an exhaustive history of PBA careers. No PBA50 or regional leaderboards are separately merged in; the profile rows themselves may have broader event scope.

Source years are kept exactly as printed. Historical split-year seasons and the missing 2012 label make calendar interpretation imperfect. The clock never jumps to the next available row; missing averages, missing years and zero denominators stay unavailable. A missing later career is not treated as a failure.

Scoring average is not adjusted for oil pattern, venue, field or era. Earnings are nominal US dollars. Event counts and paid finishes depend on the schedule. No photos, logos or article text are copied; statistical facts are attributed to the PBA.
