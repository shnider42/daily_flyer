How the comparison works

The statistic

The seasons

The first recorded season with at least 12 regular-season starts for one team sets the study clock. It is not an official rookie designation. Year two remains the next calendar season even with fewer starts. Team changes do not reset the clock. An uncertain start prevents a calculated year-two change.

What the chart can and cannot say

A rise or fall describes this measure—not a cause, overall quarterback quality or a prediction. Small passing samples can produce extreme rates. Passing statistics exclude rushing contributions. Rating and percentages are not era-adjusted; only the league-relative option uses a same-season comparison.

Charts, tables and downloads use the same selected players. Six chart panels appear per page; all selected players remain in the table and download. Panels share identical scales across pages. Missing seasons or metrics leave gaps, never zeros. Changes use unrounded source-derived values, so a printed change can differ slightly from subtraction of rounded labels.
