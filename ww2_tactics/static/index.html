      FIELD MANUAL / EXPANDED RULES

      A village worth holding.

      This saved battle keeps its existing rules. Start the next battle to unlock all the newest features.

      Americans: occupy ★ at the end of two consecutive American turns. Losing the occupying unit or moving off breaks the hold. Germans: prevent this until the battlefield's final round (8, 10, 12 or 24). Either side also wins by eliminating all enemy units.

      Overwatch · 2 actions (new battles). Reserve one reaction shot until the start of your next turn. It automatically targets the first enemy that moves into a visible hex in range, including an advance after an assault. Add +1 to the normal required roll; impossible shots do not trigger. Hits remove 1 strength and pin. Several watching units may fire at the same mover; remaining shots stop if it is eliminated. Pinning a watcher cancels its overwatch. Orange movement hexes warn of potential reactions; smoke blocks them.

      Stonebridge. Blue river hexes cannot be entered. Cross at either bridge. Bridges cost 1 action and offer no cover. Water does not block sight; the depot objective has cover.

      Role abilities · new battles

      Squads: fragmentation grenade · 2 actions. One per squad, separate from smoke. Choose a visible enemy within 2 hexes. Hit on 4+, or 5+ in woods, buildings or the objective. A hit deals 2 damage and pins; no advance or failure damage. Digging in and leader bonuses do not change the roll. Smoke blocks targeting.

      Machine guns: suppress · 2 actions. A visible, unpinned enemy within 4 hexes is guaranteed to be pinned and loses overwatch. No strength damage. Terrain cover does not prevent suppression; intervening woods, buildings and smoke still block sight.

      Leaders: rally nearby · 1 action. An unpinned leader removes pins from all adjacent friendly units without spending those units' actions. On Riverfront, rally only affects his own platoon. The leader still gives the normal shooting bonus.

      Riverfront leaders: On your feet · 2 actions. Restore 1 action to an adjacent, unpinned rifle squad or MG in the LT’s own platoon, up to 2 actions. Once per platoon per turn; cannot target leaders. Select your LT, then choose the named recipient in his orders. Mortar support remains shared across the army.

      Leaders: call mortars · 2 actions. One call per army per battle. Select a visible hex within 6 hexes. That hex and its neighbors are marked on both screens. The barrage lands at the end of the opponent's turn: all units still in the area are pinned and lose overwatch and dug-in cover, including friendly units. No strength damage. It lands even if the caller is eliminated. Pinned troops can still hold the objective, so a barrage alone does not capture it.

      Riverfront Offensive. 18×18 hexes, three bridges and 24 rounds. Each army has Alpha, Bravo and Charlie platoons, each with three squads, a leader and an MG. Use the platoon buttons to find your forces. Overview shows the entire battlefield; Detail restores readable counters. Units still act individually with 2 actions each. One mortar call per army, shared by all three leaders. Capture the north-bank rail junction using the normal two-turn hold rule.

      Give your orders

      Tap your unit. Green hexes are legal moves. Tap an enemy in range to preview a shot, then confirm Fire. End turn when ready; unused actions are lost.

      • 2 actions per unit each turn. One adjacent hex costs 1; entering woods or buildings costs 2. No stacking.
      • Fire costs 2. A server-rolled six-sided die hits on 4+. Target cover adds 1; distance over 3 hexes adds 1. A friendly leader within 1 hex subtracts 1; machine guns subtract 1. Minimum 2+. A threshold above 6 is an impossible shot.
      • Range: squad 4 hexes, MG 6, leader 3. Intervening buildings and woods block fire; you can shoot into or out of them. The square gives cover but does not block fire.
      • A hit removes 1 strength and pins. Pinned units cannot move, fire, dig, throw smoke or assault. Rally always succeeds and costs 1 action. Strength: squads/MGs 3, leaders 2. Pins persist across turns until rallied.
      • Smoke · 1 action. Each rifle squad has one grenade per match. Tap Smoke, then your hex or a neighboring hex. Smoke blocks fire into, out of and through that hex until the end of the opponent's turn. It does not block movement or assaults.
      • Dig in · 2 actions. Adds +1 to the roll enemies need to hit you, on top of terrain cover. Protection lasts until you move or assault. Digging in does not protect against assaults.
      • Assault · 2 actions. Squads and leaders may assault an adjacent enemy, even through smoke. Hit on 4+, or 3+ against a pinned enemy. Success deals 2 damage and pins; if eliminated, the attacker advances into the hex. Failure costs the attacker 1 strength and pins them. MG teams cannot initiate assaults.
      • Open information. Both players see all units. No hidden movement or tanks. Solo games use a local computer opponent with the same legal actions and dice. After End turn, its orders resolve automatically; open Computer’s last turn to review them. Swap armies using Plan next battle.
