Battle log

    FIELD MANUAL / V1

    A village worth holding.

    Americans: occupy ★ at the end of two consecutive American turns. Losing the occupying unit or moving off breaks the hold. Germans: prevent this through the end of round 8. Either side also wins by eliminating all enemy units.

    Give your orders

    Tap your unit. Green hexes are legal moves. Tap an enemy in range to preview a shot, then confirm Fire. End turn when ready; unused actions are lost.

    • 2 actions per unit each turn. One adjacent hex costs 1; entering woods or buildings costs 2. No stacking or melee.
    • Fire costs 2. A server-rolled six-sided die hits on 4+. Target cover adds 1; distance over 3 hexes adds 1. A friendly leader within 1 hex subtracts 1; machine guns subtract 1. Minimum 2+.
    • Range: squad 4 hexes, MG 6, leader 3. Intervening buildings and woods block fire; you can shoot into or out of them. The square gives cover but does not block fire.
    • A hit removes 1 strength and pins. Pinned units cannot move or fire. Rally always succeeds and costs 1 action. Strength: squads/MGs 3, leaders 2. Pins persist across turns until rallied.
    • Open information. Both players see all units. No hidden movement, reaction fire, tanks, or AI in this first version.

    An original fictional scenario inspired by tactical board games, not an official Squad Leader or Advanced Squad Leader adaptation. Early playtest: balance is not yet established.
